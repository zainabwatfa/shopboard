About the license

Every photo here is used under the Pexels License: free for personal and commercial use, with no credit required and changes allowed. We credit the photographers anyway.

Each photo was cropped to a square around the product, resized to 800 × 800 pixels and saved as WebP. Nothing else was changed.

The photos show the products only: no brand name or logo is visible, and neither the photographers nor Pexels endorse ShopBoard. This is a demo shop for a course, so nothing here is really for sale.
