Terms of use

Plain-English terms.

ShopBoard is a demo shop built for a course. These are the few things to know when you use it.

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Nothing here is for sale. You can’t buy anything on ShopBoard, and nothing you do here costs money.

Nothing is for sale

The products on this site can’t be bought. The prices are made-up examples, “Add to cart” only changes a list kept in your browser, and the checkout is a demo: it takes no payment and places no order.

No order, no contract

Because nothing is sold, using ShopBoard doesn’t create an order or a sale. Filling a cart, finishing the demo checkout or sending a demo form is not an offer to buy, and nothing on the site is an offer to sell.

Provided as it is, for learning

ShopBoard is a learning project. It is provided as it is, with no promise that it is complete, correct or always online. It may change or be taken down at any time.
