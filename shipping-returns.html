Shipping & returns

Nothing to ship.

ShopBoard is a demo shop for a course. Nothing is sold, so nothing is shipped, and there is nothing to return or refund.

This is a demo. No orders are placed and no payment is taken, so there are no deliveries, returns, exchanges or refunds.

Why this page exists

Real shops have a shipping and returns page, and a demo shop should show where it goes. Instead of making up delivery times or prices, this page lists what a real shop would explain here.

What a real shop would explain here

Example · not a real policy

  • Delivery areas and times: where the shop delivers, and how long delivery usually takes.
  • Shipping costs: what delivery costs, and whether it is ever free.
  • Order tracking: how you get a tracking link, and who to ask when a parcel is late.
  • Return window: how many days you have to send something back, in what condition, and who pays for the return postage.
  • Refund method: how the money comes back, for example to the card you paid with, and how long that takes.
  • Exchanges: whether you can swap an item for another size or colour.
  • Damaged or wrong items: what to do when something arrives broken, or isn’t what you ordered.
