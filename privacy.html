Privacy

Privacy, plainly.

ShopBoard is a demo shop for a course. It has no accounts, no analytics and no cookies of its own, and its forms send nothing. Here is exactly what is stored, and where.

Last updated: 3 November 2026

The short version

  • No accounts and no sign-in.
  • No analytics, no tracking and no ads.
  • No cookies set by this site.
  • The forms check what you type in your browser, then send nothing and save nothing.
  • Two small settings are kept in your browser, and only there.

What is stored in your browser

ShopBoard uses your browser’s localStorage for two things. They stay on your device and are never sent anywhere.

Key What it holds
shopboard-cart The products in your cart: product ids and quantities only. No prices, no personal details.
shopboard-theme Your theme choice, if you picked Light or Dark. Choosing System removes it.

How to delete it

Clear this site’s data in your browser settings (usually found under privacy, as “cookies and site data”). That removes both keys. You can also empty the cart with Clear cart on the cart page, and remove the theme choice by choosing System.

Other services

Two outside services are involved when you open this site:

  • Google Fonts. The fonts load from fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com. Like any download, this sends Google your IP address and information about your browser. See Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub Pages. GitHub hosts the site, and may log your IP address when you visit. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

The product photos are files on this site itself, so viewing them involves no other service.

Questions

Ask on GitHub issues. Issues are public, so please don’t include personal details.
