FAQ · Demo shop

Questions, answered.

Short answers about how this demo shop works. If your question isn’t here, ask it on GitHub.

Is ShopBoard a real shop?

No. ShopBoard is a demo built for a course. The products and prices are examples, so the shop has something to show: nothing is for sale and nothing ships.

Read more on the About ShopBoard page.

Can I place an order?

No. You can fill a cart and try the demo checkout, but no order is placed, no payment is taken and nothing is sent anywhere. That’s also why there is no shipping and no returns.

How is my cart saved?

In your browser’s localStorage, under the key shopboard-cart. It holds only product ids and quantities, never prices or personal details. Prices always come from the product list.

The cart belongs to this browser on this device. Another browser or device has its own cart, and a private window starts with an empty one. Clearing this site’s data in your browser settings empties the cart. It is never sent to a server.

Why does my cart update in another tab?

When the cart changes in one tab, the browser tells the other open tabs of this site (with a storage event). ShopBoard listens for it and redraws the cart and the count in the header, so every tab shows the same cart. Your theme choice is kept in step the same way.

How do I switch between light and dark?

Use the switch in the header of every page. System follows your device setting; Light and Dark override it.

Your choice is saved in this browser (under shopboard-theme) and applied before the page is drawn, so the page doesn’t flash. Choosing System removes the saved choice.

Where do the product photos come from?

They are free photos from Pexels, used under the Pexels License. The Photo credits page lists them. The photos are stored on this site, so viewing them doesn’t contact Pexels.

Is my data collected?

No. There are no accounts, no analytics and no cookies set by this site, and the forms send nothing. Your cart and theme choice stay in your browser.

Two outside services are involved when you visit: the fonts load from Google Fonts, and GitHub Pages hosts the site, so both receive your IP address. The Privacy page has the details.

Who built it, and where is the code?

ShopBoard was built as a course project. The code is on GitHub at zainabwatfa/shopboard, under the MIT License. Questions and bug reports go in GitHub issues.
